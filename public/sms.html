Text message notifications

Program: Daily Board team notifications ·

What you'll receive

Work notifications from your manager's team board: tasks assigned to you, store announcements, and occasional reminders (for example, a weekly peer-evaluation reminder). Messages come from the number above. These are internal, work-related messages only — no marketing, no promotions, and your number is never shared or sold.

How often

Message frequency varies with what your manager posts — typically a few messages a week. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. You'll get one confirmation message and nothing further.
  • Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or ask your manager.

Privacy

Your name and mobile number are kept on the team roster only so the board can reach you about your work. They are visible to your managers and are not shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or sold. Ask your manager to remove your number at any time.

How consent is collected

Team members opt in below (or tell their manager, who records it). Only people who have opted in — or who have been added to the roster by a manager after agreeing — receive messages.
